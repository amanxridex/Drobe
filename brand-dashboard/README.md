# Brand Dashboard & Onboarding Flow

The Brand Onboarding Studio and Profile system is located at:
- Source code: `app/brand-dashboard/`
- Route: `/brand-dashboard` (Onboarding wizard & studio)
- Public Profile Route: `/brand-dashboard/profile`

### Included Features:
- **Pristine Apple-Level Minimalism**: Crisp SF Pro typography, warm light surfaces (`#fbfbfd` / `#ffffff`), subtle hairline dividers (`#e5e5ea`).
- **Strict Aesthetic Constraints**: No dark shades, no pill buttons or tags, no glassomorphism blur, no unnecessary icons or noisy badges.
- **Complete Onboarding Flow**: 6 progressive steps:
  1. `01 Identity`: Brand name, tagline, industry category, established year.
  2. `02 Origins & Leadership`: Based out of / Studio location, founder name, founder role, founder quote & narrative bio.
  3. `03 Story & Tenets`: Detailed brand narrative, manifesto, 3 core pillars.
  4. `04 Visual Presence`: Cover panorama & Profile mark upload/preview with curated presets.
  5. `05 Reach & Offerings`: Flagship URL, Instagram, direct concierge inquiries, and signature product highlights.
  6. `06 Review & Publish`: Final verification and one-click publish.
- **Pre-Configured Jihi Profile**: Authentic, complete brand profile for **Jihi** (Conscious luxury skincare & mindful rituals from Telluride & Denver, CO, founded by Jenn Bernstein).
